## 1. Gemini, and what the free tier includes

The free Gemini plan is listed on Google's own plan page as $0 with a Google Account, and
includes chat, image generation and editing, Deep Research, Gemini Live, Canvas and Gems,
with access to the current Flash model and varying access to the Pro model.

- Google, Gemini plans and pricing: https://gemini.google/subscriptions/

## 2. Gemini Notebook

NotebookLM was renamed Gemini Notebook on **16 July 2026**. Google's announcement states it
is "the same standalone product, now doing more across the Google ecosystem", that notebooks
become accessible inside the Gemini app with cross app syncing, and that Google plans to
integrate notebooks into Search's AI Mode.

The same post describes the code execution capability: Google has given "every notebook a
secure cloud computer", allowing Gemini Notebook to "write and execute code natively,
helping you conduct complex data analysis grounded in your sources."

- Google, NotebookLM is now Gemini Notebook (16 July 2026):
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/notebooklm-gemini-notebook/

## 3. AI Mode in Search

AI Mode is documented as Google's AI search experience with follow up questions, links to
the web, and entry from an AI Overview. Google Lens transitions into a live AI Mode
conversation via the Live option, and context carries forward across a session.

- Google Search Help, Get AI powered responses with AI Mode in Google Search:
  https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16011537
- Google, Expanding AI Overviews and introducing AI Mode:
  https://blog.google/products/search/ai-mode-search/

## 4. Google AI Studio

Google's AI products page carries the line "Create interactive web components in Google AI
Studio with a single prompt."

Build mode, publishing and building an app from a drawing are documented by Google
directly: AI Studio's learn material includes "Build an App from a Single Mockup or Sketch",
and annotation mode lets you draw on the app preview and attach instructions to specific UI
elements. Projects can be bootstrapped into a repository and deployed to Cloud Run.

- Google, AI products: https://ai.google/products/
- Google AI Studio, Build an App from a Single Mockup or Sketch:
  https://aistudio.google.com/learn/build-app-from-drawing
- Google, Build apps in Google AI Studio:
  https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/aistudio-build-mode
- Google, Introducing vibe coding in Google AI Studio:
  https://blog.google/technology/developers/introducing-vibe-coding-in-google-ai-studio/

## 5. Firebase Studio sunset, and where the work goes

Firebase Studio is sunsetting on **22 March 2027**. New workspace creation and user signup
were disabled on **22 June 2026**. Google recommends migrating to Antigravity for a code
first agentic experience, or to Google AI Studio for web based prototyping.

- Google, Firebase Studio sunset and project migration:
  https://firebase.google.com/docs/studio/migrating-project

## 6. Gemini Code Assist: the 180,000 figure

Google's announcement post states the free individual tier offers "up to 180,000 code
completions per month", describes the capacity as "practically unlimited", and is available
in Visual Studio Code, JetBrains IDEs and GitHub. Gemini Code Assist for GitHub provides
free AI powered code reviews for public and private repositories.

The comparison in the script is Google's own framing: the post says other popular free
coding assistants offer "only 2,000 code completions per month", which Google describes as
"90 times more code completions per month". Published **25 February 2025**.

- Google, Try free Gemini Code Assist and Gemini Code Review in GitHub (25 February 2025):
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-code-assist-free/

## 7. Gemini CLI quotas

The published free tier for logging in with a Google account through Gemini Code Assist for
individuals is **1,000 model requests per user per day** and **60 model requests per user
per minute**.

- Gemini CLI docs, Quotas and Pricing:
  https://google-gemini.github.io/gemini-cli/docs/quota-and-pricing.html
- Same document in the repository:
  https://github.com/google-gemini/gemini-cli/blob/main/docs/resources/quota-and-pricing.md

## 8. Jules limits and how it works

Google's Jules usage limits list **15 daily tasks** on a rolling 24 hours and **3 concurrent
tasks** on the base plan. Jules in Pro lists 100 daily and 15 concurrent; Jules in Ultra
lists 300 daily and 60 concurrent.

Jules is documented as integrating with GitHub, running "in a virtual machine where it
clones your code, installs dependencies, and modifies files", and generating a plan you
review and approve before any code changes are made.

- Google, Jules usage limits: https://jules.google/docs/usage-limits/
- Google, Jules documentation: https://jules.google/docs

## 9. Antigravity

Google describes Antigravity as "our agent-first development platform for developers to take
an idea and turn it into a production-ready app." Antigravity 2.0 is described as "a central
home for agent interaction, allowing you to orchestrate multiple agents to execute tasks in
parallel", with "dynamic subagents for parallelized workflows, scheduled tasks for
background automation and ecosystem integrations."

The digital cities and games framing is Google's own, on the AI products page: "Build a
digital city in Google Antigravity using subagents working together" and "Build games that
improve themselves in Google Antigravity."

- Google, I/O 2026 developer highlights (19 May 2026):
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/google-io-2026-developer-highlights/
- Google, AI products: https://ai.google/products/
- Google Antigravity product page: https://antigravity.google/product

## 10. Stitch

Google's Stitch update post (**19 May 2026**) describes "transforming your design experience
into a live, collaborative partnership with the Stitch Agent, allowing you to design, stream
and steer iterations in real time", accepts text prompts, voice, existing codebases and
design files, and names three export routes directly: generate a shareable link via Google
AI Studio, export screens into Google Antigravity to plug in backend logic, or publish to
the web with Netlify.

Figma is the fourth export route named in the script. It is documented at Stitch's launch
rather than in the May 2026 update: designs "can be seamlessly pasted to Figma".

- Google, We're introducing real time design with Google Stitch (19 May 2026):
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/stitch-updates/
- Google Developers Blog, From idea to app: Introducing Stitch:
  https://developers.googleblog.com/stitch-a-new-way-to-design-uis/

## 11. Pomelli

Google's help page states: "You can currently use Pomelli at no charge" and "Users can
create several hundred image and video generations."

The same page carries the warning quoted in the script: "AI can sometimes modify fine
details of your products, like colors, logos, or textures. We recommend reviewing all
AI-generated images before publishing to ensure they accurately represent your real-world
products."

Business DNA is Pomelli's brand profile built from a business website, covering brand fonts,
colours, values and images alongside the Pomelli Catalog and assets.

- Google Labs Help, Get started with Pomelli:
  https://support.google.com/labs/answer/16715058
- Google Labs Help, Build your Business DNA and Catalog in Pomelli:
  https://support.google.com/labs/answer/17090488
- Google, Google Labs and DeepMind launch AI marketing tool Pomelli:
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/pomelli/

## 12. Google Flow

Google's Flow update post (**19 May 2026**) states Flow "evolved into an AI creative studio,
with new capabilities in video and image generation and editing, and launched in over 140
countries around the world", and introduces Flow Agent as a partner that can "plan and
reason through complex tasks with your inputs, under your control", covering brainstorming,
creation, editing, variations, batch editing and organising assets.

- Google, New agents, mobile apps and Gemini Omni for Google Flow and Google Flow Music
  (19 May 2026):
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/flow-updates/

## 13. Google Flow Music and Lyria 3 Pro

The same post states Flow Music incorporates **Lyria 3 Pro**, and describes granular
editing: users can "highlight any part of a song and make changes" without affecting the
rest of the track, "instantly rewrite or translate lyrics", "restyle the beat drop", a
covers feature that transforms the style of full songs while keeping the original melody and
structure, and directing shareable music videos conversationally.

Google's support pages document creating songs from text, image and audio prompts, and
editing, remixing, publishing, sharing by link and downloading them individually or as a
zip.

- Google, Flow updates (as above)
- Google Flow Help, Create and manage songs in Google Flow Music:
  https://support.google.com/flow/answer/17084348
- Google Flow Help, Create and manage music videos in Google Flow Music:
  https://support.google.com/flow/answer/17084421

## 14. Opal

Google describes Opal as a way to "build and share powerful AI mini apps that chain together
prompts, models, and tools, all using simple natural language and visual editing", with a
visual node based editor, hosting, and sharing so others can run the app with their own
Google account.

- Google Developers Blog, Introducing Opal: describe, create, and share your AI mini-apps:
  https://developers.googleblog.com/en/introducing-opal/
- Opal: https://opal.google/

## 15. Mixboard

Google describes Mixboard as "an experimental, AI-powered concepting board designed to help
you explore, expand and refine ideas", with an open canvas combining text, images and
generative AI, natural language editing, and one click regenerate and more like this
options. It launched in public beta in the United States.

- Google, Mixboard is a new way to visualize your ideas from Google Labs:
  https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/mixboard/
- Mixboard: https://labs.google.com/mixboard/welcome

## 16. Google Vids

Google's AI products page carries the line quoted in the script: "Create, edit and share
videos, using new AI capabilities, at no cost in Google Vids."

Google's own Workspace post is titled "Create, edit and share videos at no cost in Google
Vids", and Google states anyone with a Google account can generate video clips at no cost
using Veo 3.1.

- Google, AI products: https://ai.google/products/
- Google Workspace, Create, edit and share videos at no cost in Google Vids:
  https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/workspace/google-vids-updates-lyria-veo/
- Google Workspace, Google Vids: https://workspace.google.com/products/vids/

## Not checked

- **The $10, $20, $30 and $60 monthly figures in the opening.** These are the script's own
  illustration of a stacked subscription bill, not the price of any named product. No shot
  attaches them to a real tool, and none is presented as a published price.
- **"Two thousand completions a month used to sound generous."** This is Google's own
  comparison against unnamed competitors in its Code Assist announcement, not an
  independently verified figure for any specific competing product. The shot draws it as
  Google's stated comparison rather than as a fact about a named tool.
- **Mixboard's availability.** Google launched it in public beta in the United States. The
  narration does not state a region, and no shot draws one.
- **Google Vids at no cost, in full.** The quoted line is Google's own. The wider picture is
  more layered: Vids is generally available on Workspace Business and Enterprise plans and
  to consumers on Google AI Pro and Ultra, while free clip generation with Veo 3.1 is open
  to anyone with a Google account. The shot draws Google's quoted line and does not claim
  the whole product is free on every plan.
- **Whether any named paid tool is actually replaceable for a given viewer.** Every
  comparison in this video is about what the free baseline now covers, not a benchmark
  result. No shot draws a head to head performance figure, because none was sourced.
- **Devin, Canva, Jasper, Udio, Runway, Pika, Kling and Readwise.** These are named in the
  narration as category examples. No pricing, limit or capability figure for any of them is
  put on screen.
