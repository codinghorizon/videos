How this page works

  • Each street price is the lowest real asking price on the newest date we checked it, taken from the price checks behind our videos and our own checks of US retailers. Launch prices, auction bids and shipping are left out (Apple sells at its list price, so that is its street price). Hardware we have not checked recently is left off rather than guessed.
  • No Amazon price is ever shown on this page: Amazon's prices change by the hour, so the Check price button opens a live search instead.
  • Per GB is the street price divided by the memory the models run from: VRAM on a graphics card, unified memory on a Mac or Strix Halo machine.
  • Largest models that fit: the model's weight file is at most 85% of that memory, leaving room for context and the runtime. Long contexts need more. Each model links the sources page its file size comes from.
  • Every video's figures and sources: locallyexplains.site.
