# Grok Bot Turns One Person Into An AI Team Overnight

{% raw %}
Sources for every figure, date and product claim the video states.

## The product and its launch

**Grok Bot launched in early beta on 11 August 2026.** SpaceXAI describes it as
"your team of always-on agents. They have their own computer, work inside tools and
apps like you do, and keep working 24/7."
Source: SpaceXAI, "Introducing Grok Bot" — https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot

**Access is bundled into existing paid tiers**: SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra and
Cursor Teams Premium. Enterprise access is behind a waitlist.
Source: https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot

**SpaceXAI is the company's current name.** SpaceX acquired xAI in February 2026 in
a transaction valuing the combined company at roughly $1.25 trillion, and the
rebrand from xAI to SpaceXAI completed after the June 2026 IPO.
Sources: https://www.cnn.com/2026/02/02/tech/spacex-acquires-xai-elon-musk ·
https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/03/musk-xai-spacex-biggest-merger-ever.html ·
https://www.engadget.com/2209300/xai-now-officially-spacexai/

## The cloud computer — corrected

**All of a user's Bots share one persistent cloud computer.** The computer is scoped
to the account, not to an individual Bot. The documentation states: "All of your Bots
use the same persistent cloud computer. They share files, browser sessions, and app
logins," and "Treat a login or file placed on the computer as available to all of your
Bots."

Each Bot gets **its own screen** on that shared machine, which is what allows several
Bots to drive browser and desktop tools at the same time. A single Bot runs one
computer-use task on its screen at a time, so parallelism comes from adding Bots
rather than from one Bot multitasking.

The marketing line "they have their own computer" describes the machine belonging to
the Bots rather than to you — it is not your laptop — rather than one machine per Bot.
Sources: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview · https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq

**The documentation warns explicitly, on more than one page: "Do not use separate Bots
as a security boundary."** Separate Bots are not isolated from one another; they reach
the same credentials, files and browser sessions.
Sources: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview · https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq

## What the Bots can do

**Bots message each other and share context.** "They can message each other, share
context in threads or group chats, and pass ownership so you are not the router."
Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview

**One Bot can manage others.** Users "often run multiple Bots in parallel, with one to
manage the others," and Bots pass work and assign ownership inside group chats.
Source: https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot

**Computer use covers software with no clean API.** Bots work "across apps, tools, and
websites, including platforms with no clean API or MCP," using connectors and MCP
where they exist and computer use where they do not.
Sources: https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot · https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview

**Workflows can be learned by demonstration and scheduled.** "It can learn workflows
from live demonstration... It persists that path as a routine and can re-run it on a
schedule or on demand." A routine assigns a workflow to one Bot and tells it when to
run, on a schedule or after an event where supported.
Sources: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview · https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq

**Bots keep memory and preferences.** "They remember conversations, learn how you like
things done, and get sharper the more you work together." Named Bots keep memory,
files, browser sessions and preferences across turns.
Sources: https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot · https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview

**Approval gates exist for consequential actions.** Sensitive actions can stop for
approval, and narrow "Require Approval" rules can be set for actions such as sending,
publishing, deleting, purchasing or changing production systems.
Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq

## Internal use at the company

SpaceXAI says internal teams used Grok Bot before the public beta for **sales
outbound, marketing campaigns, office operations and bug fixes**, along with CRM
updates, invoice processing and reproducing product UI bugs.
Source: https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot

**The "last ten percent" framing is the company's own**: "There is a huge difference
between 90% done and 100% done. Most AI gets you almost there. Grok Bot can finish the
swing."
Source: https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot

## Usage and limits

**Subscriptions include a weekly usage allowance, and its size is not published.**
"Grok Bot subscriptions include weekly usage; eligible accounts can add on-demand
usage billed from model and token cost." No quota figure appears in tokens or dollars
on the product page or in the documentation.
Source: https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq

## Competing agent products

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Cursor are all shipping agent products in this period,
so Grok Bot is not operating in an empty field.
Source: https://www.unite.ai/xai-launches-grok-bot-always-on-ai-teammates-with-their-own-cloud-computers/
