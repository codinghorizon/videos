# $20 of AI a Month. One of These Two Is Wasting It.

Every figure and claim this video puts on screen, chased to a primary source. Checked
2026-08-01. Both products change their plans and their limits often, so each entry carries
the date it was checked and anything that was still moving at the time.

## The price

**Claude Pro is $20 a month.** "The Pro plan is available for $20 per month (US), with
pricing in your local currency where supported." The published pricing page also lists $17
per month on an annual subscription, billed $200 up front.
- https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8325606-what-is-the-pro-plan
- https://claude.com/pricing

**ChatGPT Plus is $20 a month.** Listed on the published plan comparison.
- https://chatgpt.com/pricing/

**$40 a month and $480 a year** for both together is arithmetic on those two list prices:
$20 + $20 = $40, and $40 x 12 = $480.

## The context window

**Claude gives 200,000 tokens on every consumer plan.** The plan comparison lists a 200k
context window against Free, Pro, Max 5x and Max 20x alike. Worth being precise about: this
is not something Pro buys over Free, it is the size Claude works at.
- https://claude.com/pricing

**ChatGPT Plus is 32,000 tokens on the default model.** The per plan context window for the
instant model on Plus and Business is 32K.
- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11909943-gpt-52-in-chatgpt

**Reasoning mode on Plus is 256,000 tokens, split 128k in and 128k out**, and only when the
reasoning model is selected by hand rather than left to the router. An earlier figure of
196k circulated widely and has been superseded.
- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11909943-gpt-52-in-chatgpt

**Six times the room** is derived: 200,000 / 32,000 = 6.25, shown rounded down to 6x.

**One 4,000 line file is about 50,000 tokens** is derived, not measured. The published rule
of thumb is roughly 4 characters per token for English text; a source line of about 45
characters is therefore around 11 to 12 tokens, and 4,000 lines lands near 48,000. This is
an order of magnitude illustration and is stated on screen as "about".
- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/4936856-what-are-tokens-and-how-to-count-them

## The limits

**Claude Pro runs on a rolling five hour session with a weekly limit over the top.**
Sessions reset every five hours, and there is "a weekly usage limit that applies across all
models".
- https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8325606-what-is-the-pro-plan

**Neither company publishes a fixed message count.** Anthropic states that how much a
session holds depends on message length, attached files, the model and the feature in use.
Any specific number of prompts per window is an estimate rather than a published figure,
which is why none appears on screen.
- https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8325606-what-is-the-pro-plan

**Claude Code is included with Pro and draws on the same pool as chat.** Usage in the IDE
and the terminal counts toward the same limits shared across Claude and Claude Code.
- https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11145838-use-claude-code-with-your-pro-or-max-plan
- https://claude.com/pricing

**ChatGPT Plus meters several things separately**, with its own allowance per model and per
feature rather than one shared budget.
- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11909943-gpt-52-in-chatgpt

**Hitting the cap on Plus swaps the model rather than stopping.** Once the message cap is
reached, conversations continue on the mini model until the window resets. That is the
difference the video draws: one plan blocks and says so, the other keeps answering on a
smaller model.
- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11909943-gpt-52-in-chatgpt

Anthropic doubled the five hour Claude Code limits on 6 May 2026 and removed the peak hours
reduction, so any comparison of how far a window stretches is a comparison of that date
onward.
- https://www.anthropic.com/news/higher-limits-spacex

## The coding agents

**Claude Code ships with Claude Pro** and runs in the terminal and in supported IDEs
including VS Code, Cursor and the JetBrains IDEs.
- https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11145838-use-claude-code-with-your-pro-or-max-plan

**Codex is included with ChatGPT plans** and runs in the terminal, in IDE extensions, in a
desktop app and in the cloud. Cloud tasks run in a sandbox preloaded with the repository and
can propose a pull request for review. Rate limits are higher on the paid plans.
- https://openai.com/index/introducing-codex/
- https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11369540-using-codex-with-your-chatgpt-plan

## The exclusives

**Image generation: ChatGPT only.** Claude has no image generation on any consumer plan.

**Video generation: ChatGPT only**, through Sora. Claude has no video generation.

**Custom GPTs: ChatGPT only.** A library of assistants built and published by other people,
with no equivalent on Claude.
- https://chatgpt.com/pricing/

**Apps built and run inside the conversation: Claude only.** Claude renders and runs
interactive artifacts inside the chat itself.
- https://claude.com/pricing

**Connectors are on both, and this is the one most comparisons get wrong.** ChatGPT connects
to a large catalogue of third party apps including Slack, Google Drive, SharePoint, GitHub
and Atlassian. Claude connects to Google Workspace, Slack and remote MCP connectors, and
supports custom ones. The difference is breadth, not presence, which is why the video says
both do it.
- https://claude.com/pricing
- https://chatgpt.com/pricing/

**Voice is on both.** Claude's voice mode is included for Pro at no extra cost, is available
in the mobile apps, and was extended in 2026 to more languages, push to talk, and to the
Opus and Sonnet models. It is not a ChatGPT exclusive and is not presented as one.
- https://claude.com/pricing

## What is not verified

- No specific number of messages, prompts or tasks per window is claimed for either plan,
  because neither company publishes one as a fixed figure.
- The per feature percentages shown on the quota gauges are an illustration of separate
  allowances existing, not published values for any particular feature.
- The 4,000 line file and its token count are derived from a published characters per token
  rule of thumb and will vary with the language and the code.
- Model names and per plan context windows on both sides move frequently. Everything here
  reflects the published plan documentation as of 2026-08-01.
- The relative quality of the two products' answers is not assessed anywhere in this video,
  only what each plan includes and what it does when the allowance runs out.
